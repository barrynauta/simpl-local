# 6.10 - The Governance Authority aggregates DOME Marketplace offerings with the other search results

## Canonical source
[https://simpl-programme.ec.europa.eu/book-page/610-governance-authority-aggregates-dome-marketplace-offerings-other-search-results](https://simpl-programme.ec.europa.eu/book-page/610-governance-authority-aggregates-dome-marketplace-offerings-other-search-results)

## Description

Simpl-Open shall support the Governance Authority in aggregating the offerings retrieved from the DOME Marketplace with the resource descriptions returned from the other selected search scopes and providing the combined search results to the Consumer.

Issue ID: SIMPL-6762. Status at time of capture (5 October 2026): Proposed.

None published at the time of capture.
