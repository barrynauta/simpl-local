# 6.9 - The Governance Authority forwards the query of the Consumer on the DOME Marketplace catalogue

## Canonical source
[https://simpl-programme.ec.europa.eu/book-page/69-governance-authority-forwards-query-consumer-dome-marketplace-catalogue](https://simpl-programme.ec.europa.eu/book-page/69-governance-authority-forwards-query-consumer-dome-marketplace-catalogue)

## Description

Simpl-Open shall support the Governance Authority in forwarding the Consumer's search query to the DOME Marketplace catalogue and receiving the offerings that match the search criteria.

Issue ID: SIMPL-6761. Status at time of capture (5 October 2026): Proposed.

None published at the time of capture.
