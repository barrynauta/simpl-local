## Source hierarchy

When sources disagree, the first one in this list wins. It applies to every decision.

1. **These decisions.** They are the only place where a conflict is settled for the build.
2. **The newest live story for a step.** The October 2026 R17 stories (SIMPL-35440 to 35889) win over older stories for the same step, and a story marked "Replaced" or "New proposed: Replaces" loses to its replacement. Why: they name the current technical architecture and the published endpoints, and the older ones say they are replaced.
3. **Acceptance criteria for behaviour** (what happens, in which order, what is stored, which callback is made). Why: they are what a tester checks.
4. **The OpenAPI for the wire format** (method, path, status code, schema and enum names) where it defines the operation. Story wire details count only for operations that are not in an OpenAPI. Why: the OpenAPI is the published contract that other components call.
5. **Technical notes in the stories** for the order of internal steps and the collaborators.
6. **The CDM** for ownership of concepts (external or own). It is in review, so it does not override 2 to 5.
7. **The business process** for meaning and for the business terms. For names, the business process comes first (DDD-01): business terms are used in tests and new names, published wire names stay as they are with a mapping in the glossary.

Where all sources are silent, the decision takes what is simplest to build and test, and says so.

| D12 | One state machine for the usage contract | The Contract Manager holds one usage contract record per contractNegotiationId and side, with one state machine; the offer is its first phase. Some of these refine earlier decisions, and the writers read both together:

- D31 overrides the success outcome of D7 (the Provider record goes to Signed, wire FINALIZING, and the 201 says FINALIZING; Active comes only with the Consumer's confirmation) and refines D12 (Signed now exists on both sides; Requested to Active on the Provider side is replaced by Requested to Signed and Signed to Active).
- D32 refines D12 (new exits: Signed to Closed by timeout or relayed rejection on both sides, Requested to Closed by relayed rejection, Active to Closed on the Consumer side by relayed rejection) and D8 (the Consumer can also close without its own validation).
- D33 refines D5 and D7 (a retry reuses the issued VC).
- D34 refines D5 (a repeated accept or reject gives 200, not 409; the 409 carries currentStatus), D13 (the scheduler stops on 409 and 404) and D14 (timeout and alignment in contract-ui-be).
- D35 refines D11 (two more notification types, sent to the local participant).
- D36 and D37 refine D3 (which records count as existing) and D7 and D9 (a new rejection reason EXISTING_USAGE_CONTRACT).

| D31 | Provider usage contract waits for the Consumer's confirmation | On the Provider side, a successful validateAndIssue (D7) no longer ends in Active. This is how the Consumer learns that the Provider rejected (D7 REJECTED, D36) or timed out. On the Consumer side the value true answers 400: the Provider's success reaches the Consumer through PUT /verifications (D8). Active to Closed on the Consumer side is the compensating step for a Provider that closed its own record; it is the only exit from Active in this build. ## Questions for the owners, by impact

Highest impact first: the first ones change what is built and tested on the main path.

1. **Target flow (D1).** Confirm that the R17 stories (SIMPL-35526, 35584, 35660, 35677, 35440, 35692) are the target, and close SIMPL-19824, 19829, 22690 and 19934. Keep SIMPL-19933 and 21948 only for their checks and reasons.
2. **Both sides end in the same state (D31, D32).** Confirm that the Provider's usage contract becomes active only after the Consumer's confirmation, that the Connector relays the other side's /isValid result to its own Contract Manager through the new POST /agreements/{contractNegotiationId}/validationResult/{isValid}, the Signed timeout (default one day), and that a Consumer usage contract may go from Active to Closed when the Provider closed its side.
3. **One state machine (D12, D13).** Confirm the states Requested, Signed, Active, Closed (and the reserved lifecycle states), their mapping to CREATED, FINALIZING, FINALIZED, TERMINATED, the new EXPIRED value in contract-ui-be, and the rename of APPROVED to ACCEPTED.
4. **Duplicate and expired usage contracts (D36, D37).** Confirm that an open request (Requested or Signed) blocks a new one for the same Consumer and resource, the new reason EXISTING_USAGE_CONTRACT, and that a usage contract past the validUntil of its VC no longer counts as existing. Does an expired usage contract need its own state, and must billing and access stop at validUntil?
5. **Operations not built (D26).** Remove POST .../issue, PATCH /agreements/{contractAgreementId}/definitions/{contractDefinitionId}/status, GET /agreements/{contractAgreementId}/file, PUT /contractOffers/{contractNegotiationId}/update and the Contract Manager POST /contractOffers/{contractNegotiationId} from the next OpenAPI version. Does POST /agreements/search serve the overview of usage contracts after acceptance? If yes, it needs a story.
6. **Lifecycle after Active (D15, accepted risk).** Which backend owns revoke, suspend, reactivate and terminate, through which channel a change reaches the other participant, and how resource access is removed and restored.
7. **Provider validate and sign (D7).** Add validateAndIssue to the OpenAPI: synchronous, 201 on success, 422 with a rejection reason, 500 on a technical error. Should a Provider-side rejection call /isValid, /signed or both?
8. **Terms in the offer (D38, accepted risk).** Which terms the usage contract offer shows and signs (policy, clause hashes, billing schema), and from which API the Contract Manager gets them.
9. **Retries and alignment (D33, D34).** Document in the OpenAPI the 200 for a repeated accept or reject and the currentStatus member of the 409; confirm the 30 second timeout of contract-ui-be. VC Issuer owner: is there a revocation operation for a usage contract VC that never took effect?
10. **Side of /create and of the existence check (D4, D3).** Confirm /create on the Consumer side, and say on which side the existence check runs (BP 7.2.2 and SIMPL-35858 disagree). Is SIMPL-32509 withdrawn?
11. **Wire format of accept and validation (D5, D8).** Confirm PATCH with 200 for accept and reject, and PUT with 200 and an asynchronous result for /verifications; can the VC body of VerifyCredentialRequest go?
12. **Identity and names (D19, D20, D21).** Confirm contractNegotiationId as the identity of the usage contract and the EDC agreement id as contractAgreementId; plan the rename of the CDM class to UsageContract and of the paths.
13. **Align the OpenAPI files with the decisions (D27, D28, D29, D30).** Add the response codes per operation and the required lists of D27, remove the 404 of GET /contractOffers, make the PUT /verifications body optional, add the ids and createdAt to the contract-ui-be ContractOffer, use the E5 problem types in every example with instance as uri-reference, align the contract-ui-be examples with the schema (no lastUpdatedDate, offerUrl to the frontend), and declare date-time in UTC for every timestamp. Clean-up for the same version (minors): remove the 403 responses and name the gateway in the security description (P-008), business info.description (P-009), realistic examples (P-010), parameter description and declared tags (P-011), notes for the planned renames (P-012); the adapter points go to its owners (P-013).
14. **Validation rules (D9).** Confirm the Provider-side reason codes, FAILED without retry, the duplicate rule (409 on an Active or Closed record) and the VC validity dates as the contract validity period.
15. **Outcome notifications (D35).** Confirm that each side notifies its own participant when a usage contract becomes Active or Closed, and whether the reason must also reach the other party (that needs a reason in the Connector callbacks).
16. **Limits on automatic signing (D40, accepted risk).** Is a rate limit per Consumer and resource needed, and where? May a Provider hold automatic signing for approval? Must the accepting user be recorded, and through which gateway header?
17. **New endpoints (D10, D17).** Add GET /verifications/{contractNegotiationId}/vc and GET /audits/usageContractStatusCounts to the OpenAPI; describe the audit rule package.
18. **Overview data (D14, D23).** Which API serves usage contracts after acceptance, and where resource description id, resource name and Provider name come from.
19. **Notification Service contract (D11, accepted risk).** Publish the integration contract (topic or endpoint, payload, retry).
20. **Wallet failures (D6).** Drop 'specify another secure location' from SIMPL-19927 and 19938 and remove the duplicate criteria in SIMPL-19938.
21. **Countersigned usage contract in the Consumer wallet (D41).** Must the Consumer store the Provider's VC in its wallet? If yes, the wallet needs a key per VC and D10 must say which VC it returns.
22. **Out of this build (D16, D18, D2).** Publish the consent status source (SIMPL-31047) and the upload API (SIMPL-28846); confirm that SIMPL-21924 belongs to the catalogue and that the Contract Orchestrator is gone.
23. **CDM work (D24).** Complete and approve the two Contract Management CDMs, mark the external classes with their owners, and draw the context map.
