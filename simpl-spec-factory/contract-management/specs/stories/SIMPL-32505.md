# SIMPL-32505: A Consumer accepts a usage contract offer via UI
- Status: New Product Backlog Item
- Type: Story
- Created: 2026-09-03, updated: 2026-09-07
- Source: https://jira.simplprogramme.eu/browse/SIMPL-32505 (collected 2026-10-29)

## Description and acceptance criteria

New proposed: Replaces SIMPL-22690

**As a** Consumer\
**I want** to to accept a usage contract offer I have reviewed in my contract manager via UI\
**So that** I can proceed with the signing and finalisation of the usage contract

Acceptance Criteria

AC1 - A Consumer accepts a usage contract offer via UI

**Given** I am authenticated as a Consumer end user\
**And** I have a role authorising contract management\
**And** I have requested a usage contract offer for a resource\
**When** I retrieve the usage contract offer in my contract manager\
**Then** I can click on a button to accept the usage contract offer

AC2 - A Consumer successfully finishes accepting a usage contract offer via UI

**Given** I am authenticated as a Consumer end user\
**And** I have a role authorising contract management\
**And** I have requested a usage contract offer for a resource\
**And** I have retrieved the usage contract offer in my contract manager\
**When** I click on the button to accept the usage contract offer\
**Then** I see the usage contract with the updated status "Accepted" in the contract manager

AC3 - A Consumer cannot accept a usage contract offer that is accepted, active, rejected, suspended or terminated via UI

**Given** I am authenticated as a Consumer end user\
**And** I have a role authorising contract management\
**And** I have requested a usage contract offer for a resource\
**When** I retrieve a usage contract (offer) in my contract manager with a status accepted, active, rejected, suspended or terminated\
**Then** I cannot click on a button to accept the usage contract offer
