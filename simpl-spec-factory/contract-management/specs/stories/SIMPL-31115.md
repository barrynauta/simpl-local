# SIMPL-31115: A Provider suspends a usage contract by providing a reason in their contract manager UI
- Status: New Product Backlog Item
- Type: Story
- Created: 2026-08-03, updated: 2026-10-26
- Source: https://jira.simplprogramme.eu/browse/SIMPL-31115 (collected 2026-10-29)

## Description and acceptance criteria

**As a** Provider,\
**I want** to suspend a selected usage contract via UI\
**So that** I have the flexibility to temporarily remove access to a resource for the specific reasons I have provided (e.g. breach of a usage policy).

Acceptance Criteria

**AC1 - A Provider wants to suspend of one of their active usage contracts via UI**

**Given** I am authenticated as a Provider\
**And** I have a role authorising the management of usage contracts\
**And** I am retrieving my usage contracts in the contract manager UI\
**And** I select one of my active usage contracts\
**When** I click on the actions of that usage contract\
**Then** I can choose an action to suspend the usage contract

**AC2 - *A Provider suspends of one of their active usage contracts via UI***

**Given** I am authenticated as a Provider\
**And** I have a role authorising the management of usage contracts\
**And** I have retrieved my usage contracts in the contract manager UI\
**And** I have selected one of my active usage contracts\
**When** I select the action to suspend the usage contract and I provide a reason for the suspension\
**Then** the usage contract is suspend\
**And** I can observe that the status of the usage contract has changed to 'Suspended' in my contract manager UI

**AC3 - *A Consumer observes via UI that of one of their active usage contracts was suspended by the Provider***

**Given** I am authenticated as a Consumer\
**And** I have a role authorising the management of usage contracts\
**And** the Provider has suspended one of my active usage contracts\
**When** I retrieve my usage contracts in the contract manager UI\
**Then** I can observe that the status of the usage contract has changed to 'Suspended'
