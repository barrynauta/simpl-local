# SIMPL-31053: A Provider or Consumer requests the revocation of a usage contract in their contract manager UI
- Status: FOR SC TO RESPOND
- Type: Story
- Created: 2026-07-31, updated: 2026-10-26
- Source: https://jira.simplprogramme.eu/browse/SIMPL-31053 (collected 2026-10-29)

## Description and acceptance criteria

**As a** Provider or Consumer\
**I want** to request the revocation of a selected usage contract via UI\
**So that** I have the flexibility to discontinue my contractual obligations for the specific reasons I have provided.

Requesting Participant: The Provider or Consumer that requests the revocation of the usage contract\
Responding Participant: The Provider or Consumer that responds to the revocation request of the requesting Participant

Acceptance Criteria

AC1 - A Provider or Consumer wants to request the revocation of one of their usage contracts via UI

**Given** I am authenticated as a Provider or Consumer (requesting Participant)\
**And** I have a role authorising the management of usage contracts\
**And** I am retrieving my usage contracts in the contract manager UI\
**When** I click on the actions of an active usage contract\
**Then** I can choose an action to request the revocation of the usage contract\
**And** I am requested to provide a reason for the revocation request

AC2 - A Provider or Consumer requests the revocation of one of their usage contracts via UI

**Given** I am authenticated as a Provider or Consumer (requesting Participant)\
**And** I have a role authorising the management of usage contracts\
**And** I have retrieved my usage contracts in the contract manager UI\
**When** I select the action to request the revocation of one of my usage contracts and I provide a reason\
**Then** I can observe that the status of the usage contract has changed to 'Revocation requested' in my contract manager UI

AC3 - The responding Participant observes the results of the revocation request by the requesting Participant via UI (Accept case)

**Given** I am authenticated as a Provider or Consumer (responding Participant)\
**And** I have a role authorising the management of usage contracts\
**And** the requesting Participant has requested the revocation of one of my usage contracts\
**When** I retrieve my usage contracts in the contract manager UI\
**Then** I can observe that the status of the usage contract has changed to 'Revocation requested'
