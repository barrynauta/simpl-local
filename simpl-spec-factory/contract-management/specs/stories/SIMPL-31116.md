# SIMPL-31116: A Consumer requests the reactivation of a suspended usage contract in their contract manager UI
- Status: New Product Backlog Item
- Type: Story
- Created: 2026-08-03, updated: 2026-10-26
- Source: https://jira.simplprogramme.eu/browse/SIMPL-31116 (collected 2026-10-29)

## Description and acceptance criteria

**As a** Consumer\
**I want** to reactivate a suspended usage contract via UI\
**So that** I can gain access to the resource again.

Acceptance Criteria

**AC1 - A Consumer wants to reactivate one of their suspended usage contracts via UI**

**Given** I am authenticated as a Consumer\
**And** I have a role authorising the management of usage contracts\
**And** I am retrieving my usage contracts in the contract manager UI\
**And** the status of one of my usage contracts is 'Suspended'\
**When** I click on the actions of the usage contract with status 'Suspended'\
**Then** I can choose an action to request the reactivation of the usage contract

**AC2 -** *A Consumer requests the reactivation of one of their suspended usage contracts via UI*

**Given** I am authenticated as a Consumer\
**And** I have a role authorising the management of usage contracts\
**And** I have retrieved my usage contracts in the contract manager UI\
**And** the status of one of my usage contracts is 'Suspended'\
**When** I select the action to request the reactivation of the suspended usage contract\
**And** I provide a justification for the reactivation request\
**And** I can observe that the status of the usage contract has changed to 'Reactivation requested' in my contract manager UI

**AC3 -** **A Provider observes the results of the reactivation request by the Consumer via UI**

**Given** I am authenticated as a Provider\
**And** I have a role authorising the management of usage contracts\
**And** the Consumer has requested the reactivation of a suspended usage contract\
**When** I retrieve my usage contracts in the contract manager UI\
**Then** I can observe that the status of the usage contract has changed to 'Reactivation requested'
