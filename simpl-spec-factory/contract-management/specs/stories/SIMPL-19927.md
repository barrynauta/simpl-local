# SIMPL-19927: Consumer stores signed usage contract securely in digital wallet
- Status: DevTeam PBI is ready for Implementation check
- Type: Story
- Created: 2025-10-24, updated: 2026-08-20
- Source: https://jira.simplprogramme.eu/browse/SIMPL-19927 (collected 2026-10-29)

## Description and acceptance criteria

Why: Store signed usage contract in secure, machine-readable format for reference purposes

What:

**As a** Consumer,\
**I want** to store the signed usage contract in a digital wallet\
**So that** it can be saved in a secure location in machine-readable format for future reference.

Acceptance Criteria

**AC1 - Storing contract successfully in digital wallet:**\
**Given** I am authenticated as a Consumer\
**And** I receive the fully-signed usage contract received from the Provider\
**And** I validate the integrity and the authenticity of Provider's signature\
**When** my digital wallet is ready for use\
**Then** I store securely the fully-signed usage contract in my digital wallet\
**And** the fully-signed usage contract is archived\
**And** it is available in a machine-readable format for reference purposes.\
**And** its access is restricted to authorized roles.

**AC2 -  Digital wallet is unavailable:**\
**Given** I am authenticated as a Consumer\
**And** I receive the fully-signed usage contract received from the Provider\
**And** I validate the integrity and the authenticity of Provider's signature\
**And** my digital wallet is not available\
**When** I archive the fully-signed usage contract in my digital wallet for reference purposes\
**Then** I receive an error message stating "Secure storage location unavailable"\
**And** I am requested to specify another secure location for archiving the contract.

**AC3 - Digital wallet is full:**\
**Given** I am authenticated as a Consumer\
**And** I receive the fully-signed usage contract received from the Provider\
**And** I validate the integrity and the authenticity of Provider's signature\
**And** my digital wallet is lacking available capacity\
**When** I archive the fully-signed usage contract in my digital wallet for reference purposes\
**Then** I receive an error message stating "Secure storage location is full"\
**And** I am requested to specify another secure location for archiving the contract.

**AC4 - Digital wallet is corrupted:**\
**Given** I am authenticated as a Consumer\
**And** I receive the fully-signed usage contract received from the Provider\
**And** I validate the integrity and the authenticity of Provider's signature\
**And** my digital wallet is corrupted\
**When** I archive the fully-signed usage contract in my digital wallet for reference purposes\
**Then** I receive an error message stating "Secure storage location is corrupted"\
**And** I am requested to specify another secure location for archiving the contract.

Technical Info:\
* Machine-readable format should be standardized across all contract type, e.g. JSON LD\
* Use protocol: W3C Verifiable Credentials\
* Ensure secure transmission of signed contract\
* Signed contract format for wallet compatibility: convert to required format (e.g., JSON-LD for VC, XML for eIDAS)\
* Handle delivery confirmation and error states: Log success/failure, notify user of result

Additional Notes: This Story (about archiving contracts at Consumer side) is similar to [SIMPL-19938](https://example.com) (at Provider side).
