# SIMPL-21963: Consumer delivers the signed usage contract offer to the Provider
- Status: PBI Defined
- Type: Story
- Created: 2025-12-08, updated: 2026-10-28
- Source: https://jira.simplprogramme.eu/browse/SIMPL-21963 (collected 2026-10-29)

## Description and acceptance criteria

Why

To allow the Provider to continue the usage contract establishment process after the Consumer has signed the usage contract offer.

What

**As a** Provider,\
**I want** to receive the Consumer’s signed usage contract offer,\
**So that** I can validate the Consumer signature and continue with Provider-side signing.

Scope

This story covers the step where the Consumer-signed usage contract offer becomes available to the Provider as part of the usage contract establishment process.

The story does not require delivery of one final fully signed contract artefact. The final usage contract is completed only after both Consumer and Provider signing steps are completed.

The exact technical delivery mechanism must follow the agreed Contract Manager / Connector integration flow.

Acceptance Criteria

AC1 Consumer-signed offer is available to Provider

**Given** the Consumer has signed the usage contract offer\
**When** the signing result is provided to the Provider\
**Then** the Provider can access the Consumer-signed usage contract offer or signing evidence.

AC2 Provider can continue validation

**Given** the Consumer-signed usage contract offer is available to the Provider\
**When** the Provider reviews the related usage contract establishment process\
**Then** the Provider can continue with validation of the Consumer signature.

AC3 Process does not continue if Consumer signing is missing

**Given** the Consumer signing step is not completed or cannot be confirmed\
**When** the Provider attempts to continue the process\
**Then** the Provider-side validation and signing process cannot continue as successful.

AC4 Delivery is traceable

**Given** the Consumer-signed usage contract offer is provided to the Provider\
**When** the process is reviewed\
**Then** there is sufficient evidence to confirm that the Consumer signing step was completed and made available to the Provider.

Technical note

The technical delivery mechanism must follow the agreed Contract Manager / Connector integration flow.

This story must not introduce a separate requirement to transfer one final fully signed contract artefact from Consumer to Provider.

Detailed API, event, integration, correlationId and retry behaviour should be covered by the relevant technical implementation / integration stories.

Verification notes for PSO

This story is business-level and does not introduce a new dedicated UI endpoint.

The Consumer-signed usage contract offer / signing evidence should be verified through the agreed Contract Manager / Connector integration flow and the existing Contract API implementation.
