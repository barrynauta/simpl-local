# SIMPL-31076: The responding Participant responds to a revocation request of a usage contract in their contract manager UI
- Status: FOR SC TO RESPOND
- Type: Story
- Created: 2026-08-03, updated: 2026-10-26
- Source: https://jira.simplprogramme.eu/browse/SIMPL-31076 (collected 2026-10-29)

## Description and acceptance criteria

**As a** responding Participant\
**I want** to respond to a revocation request for a usage contract via UI\
**So that** I have the flexibility to dispute any revocations that are requested for my usage contracts.

Requesting Participant: The Provider or Consumer that requests the revocation of the usage contract\
Responding Participant: The Provider or Consumer that responds to the revocation request of the requesting Participant

Acceptance Criteria

AC1 - The responding Participant wants to respond to the revocation request of one of their usage contracts via UI

**Given** I am authenticated as a Provider or Consumer (responding Participant)\
**And** I have a role authorising the management of usage contracts\
**And** I am retrieving my usage contracts in the contract manager UI\
**And** the status of one of my usage contracts is 'Revocation requested'\
**When** I click on the actions of the usage contract with status 'Revocation requested'\
**Then** I can choose an action to respond to the revocation of the usage contract

AC2 - The responding Participant accepts the revocation request of one of their usage contracts via UI

**Given** I am authenticated as a Provider or Consumer (responding Participant)\
**And** I have a role authorising the management of usage contracts\
**And** I have retrieved my usage contracts in the contract manager UI\
**And** the status of one of my usage contracts is 'Revocation requested'\
**When** I select the action to respond with 'accept' to the revocation request\
**Then** the usage contract is revoked\
**And** I can observe that the status of the usage contract has changed to 'Revoked' in my contract manager UI

AC3 - The responding Participant denies the revocation request of one of their usage contracts via UI

**Given** I am authenticated as a Provider or Consumer (responding Participant)\
**And** I have a role authorising the management of usage contracts\
**And** I have retrieved my usage contracts in the contract manager UI\
**And** the status of one of my usage contracts is 'Revocation requested'\
**When** I select the action to respond with 'deny' to the revocation request and I provide a justification for the denial\
**Then** I can observe that the status of the status of the usage contract has changed back to 'Active' in my contract manager UI

AC4 - The requesting Participant observes the results of the response to the revocation request via UI (Accept case)

**Given** I am authenticated as a Provider or Consumer (requesting Participant)\
**And** I have a role authorising the management of usage contracts\
**And** the responding Participant has responded to my revocation request with an 'Accept' decision\
**When** I retrieve my usage contracts in the contract manager UI\
**Then** I can observe that the status of the usage contract has changed to 'Revoked'

AC4 - The requesting Participant observes the results of the response to the revocation request via UI (Reject case)

**Given** I am authenticated as a Provider or Consumer (requesting Participant)\
**And** I have a role authorising the management of usage contracts\
**And** the responding Participant has responded to my revocation request with a 'Reject' decision\
**When** I retrieve my usage contracts in the contract manager UI\
**Then** I can observe that the status of the usage contract has changed to 'Active'
