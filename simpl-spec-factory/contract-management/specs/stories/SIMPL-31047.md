# SIMPL-31047: A Provider chooses the action to update the contract consent assertion status of a selected usage contract in their contract manager UI
- Status: PBI For DG Review
- Type: Story
- Created: 2026-07-31, updated: 2026-10-28
- Source: https://jira.simplprogramme.eu/browse/SIMPL-31047 (collected 2026-10-29)

## Description and acceptance criteria

**As a** Provider\
**I want** to trigger the update of the contract consent assertion status related to a selected usage contract via UI\
**So that** I can ensure that the access to the resource is aligned the contract consent status.

Acceptance Criteria

AC1 - A Provider decides to update the contract consent assertion status of one of their usage contracts via UI

**Given** I am authenticated as a Provider\
**And** I have a role authorising the management of usage contracts and consent\
**And** I am retrieving my usage contracts in the contract manager UI\
**When** I check the contract consent status of a usage contract\
**Then** I can choose an action to update the contract consent status

AC2 - A Provider performs the action to update the contract consent assertion status of one of their usage contracts via UI

**Given** I am authenticated as a Provider\
**And** I have a role authorising the management of usage contracts and consent\
**And** I am retrieving my usage contracts in the contract manager UI\
**When** I click on the action to update the contract consent status\
**Then** I am redirected to the consent manager to perform the change

AC3 - A Provider observes the change of the contract consent status in their contract manager UI

**Given** I am authenticated as a Provider\
**And** I have a role authorising the management of usage contracts and consent\
**And** I have selected a usage contract in the contract manager UI\
**And** I clicked on the action to update the contract consent status\
**When** I have performed the update of the contract consent assertion status in the consent manager UI\
**Then** I can observe the change of the contract consent status for the usage contract in the contract manager UI

AC4 - A Provider cannot perform the action to update the contract consent status if contract consent is not defined on the selected usage contract UI

**Given** I am authenticated as a Provider\
**And** I have a role authorising the management of usage contracts and consent\
**And** I am retrieving my usage contracts in the contract manager UI\
**When** I look at the actions related to a usage contract for which contract consent is not defined\
**Then** I cannot choose an action to update the contract consent status
