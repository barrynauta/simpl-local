# SIMPL-31118: A Provider terminates a usage contract by providing a justification related to the termination conditions in their contract manager UI
- Status: New Product Backlog Item
- Type: Story
- Created: 2026-08-03, updated: 2026-10-26
- Source: https://jira.simplprogramme.eu/browse/SIMPL-31118 (collected 2026-10-29)

## Description and acceptance criteria

**As a** Provider\
**I want** to terminate a usage contract via UI\
**So that** I can remove access to a resource when the termination conditions from the usage contract are met.

Acceptance Criteria

AC1 - A Provider wants to terminate of one of their active usage contract via UI

**Given** I am authenticated as a Provider\
**And** I have a role authorising the management of usage contracts\
**And** I am retrieving my usage contracts in the contract manager UI\
**And** I select one of my usage contracts that is not revoked or terminated\
**When** I click on the actions of that usage contract\
**Then** I can choose an action to terminate the usage contract

AC2 - A Provider terminates of one of their active usage contract via UI

**Given** I am authenticated as a Provider\
**And** I have a role authorising the management of usage contracts\
**And** I have retrieved my usage contracts in the contract manager UI\
**And** I have selected one of my usage contracts that is not revoked or terminated\
**When** I select the action to terminate the usage contract and I provide a justification related to the termination conditions of the usage contract\
**Then** the usage contract is terminated\
**And** I can observe that the status of the usage contract has changed to 'Terminated' in my contract manager UI

AC3 - A Consumer observes via UI that of one of their active usage contract was terminated by the Provider

**Given** I am authenticated as a Consumer\
**And** I have a role authorising the management of usage contracts\
**And** the Provider has terminated one of my active usage contracts\
**When** I retrieve my usage contracts in the contract manager UI\
**Then** I can observe that the status of the usage contract has changed to 'Terminated'
