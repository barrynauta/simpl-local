# SIMPL-31117: A Provider reactivates a suspended usage contract in their contract manager UI
- Status: New Product Backlog Item
- Type: Story
- Created: 2026-08-03, updated: 2026-10-26
- Source: https://jira.simplprogramme.eu/browse/SIMPL-31117 (collected 2026-10-29)

## Description and acceptance criteria

**As a** Provider,\
**I want** to reactivate a suspended usage contract via UI\
**So that** I can make the resource available again to the Consumer after a suspension of the usage contract.

Acceptance Criteria

**AC1 - A Provider wants to reactivate one of the usage contracts they suspended via UI**

**Given** I am authenticated as a Provider\
**And** I have a role authorising the management of usage contracts\
**And** I am retrieving my usage contracts in the contract manager UI\
**And** the status of one of my usage contracts is 'Suspended' or 'Reactivation requested'\
**When** I click on the actions of one of those usage contracts\
**Then** I can choose an action to reactivate the usage contract

**AC2 - A Provider reactivates one of the usage contracts they suspended via UI**

**Given** I am authenticated as a Provider\
**And** I have a role authorising the management of usage contracts\
**And** I have retrieved my usage contracts in the contract manager UI\
**And** the status of one of my usage contracts is 'Suspended' or 'Reactivation requested'\
**When** I select the action to reactivate one of those usage contracts\
**Then** I can observe that the status of the usage contract has changed to 'Active' in my contract manager UI

**AC3 - A Consumer observes the results of the reactivation by the Provider via UI**

**Given** I am authenticated as a Consumer\
**And** I have a role authorising the management of usage contracts\
**And** the Provider has reactivated a suspended usage contract\
**When** I retrieve my usage contracts in the contract manager UI\
**Then** I can observe that the status of the usage contract has changed to 'Active'
