# 7.5 - A Consumer signs, stores and provide the usage contract offer

## Canonical source
[https://simpl-programme.ec.europa.eu/book-page/75-consumer-signs-stores-and-provide-usage-contract-offer](https://simpl-programme.ec.europa.eu/book-page/75-consumer-signs-stores-and-provide-usage-contract-offer)

## Description

Simpl-Open shall support the Consumer to:

- Sign the usage contract offer.
- Store the signed usage contract offer and provide it to the Provider.

## Detailed Requirements

- **7.5.1 – A Consumer signs the usage contract offer** — Simpl-Open shall support the Consumer via UI and API to sign the usage contract offer. [Details](https://simpl-programme.ec.europa.eu/book-page/751-consumer-signs-usage-contract-offer)
- **7.5.2 – A Consumer stores the signed usage contract offer and provide it to the Provider** — Simpl-Open shall support the Consumer to automatically store and forward the signed offer. [Details](https://simpl-programme.ec.europa.eu/book-page/752-consumer-stores-signed-usage-contract-offer-and-provide-it-provider)
