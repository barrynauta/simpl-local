# SIMPL-18165: Download and view full details of the newest vocabulary version in the UI
- Status: Ready for DG Acceptance
- Type: Story
- Created: 2025-09-10, updated: 2026-09-25
- Source: https://jira.simplprogramme.eu/browse/SIMPL-18165 (collected 2026-10-27)

## Description and acceptance criteria

**As a** Governance Authority\
**I want** to download and view the complete details of the newest vocabulary version in the UI\
**So that** I can review the latest content

Acceptance Criteria

**Given** I am authenticated as a Governance Authority with permission to manage vocabularies\
**And** I am on the Vocabulary Management page\
**When** I choose the download action for a vocabulary\
**Then** I can download the newest version of that vocabulary\
**And** I can review its full details
