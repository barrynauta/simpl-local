# SIMPL-18195: Role-based access control for vocabulary management UI
- Status: Ready for DG Acceptance
- Type: Story
- Created: 2025-09-10, updated: 2026-08-12
- Source: https://jira.simplprogramme.eu/browse/SIMPL-18195 (collected 2026-10-27)

## Description and acceptance criteria

**As a** Governance Authority,\
**I want** vocabulary management actions in the UI to be accessible to authorised users only,\
**So that** sensitive vocabulary management operations remain secure and compliant.

## Acceptance Criteria

**Given** I am authenticated as a Governance Authority user with a vocabulary management role\
**When** I access vocabulary management functionality in the UI\
**Then** all related actions such as create, update version, and change vocabulary status are enabled and accessible

**Given** I am authenticated as a Governance Authority user without the vocabulary management role\
**When** I access vocabulary management functions in the UI\
**Then** restricted actions are hidden or disabled\
**And** attempts to perform restricted actions display an authorization error message

**Given** I am not authenticated\
**When** I attempt to access vocabulary management functionality in the UI\
**Then** access is denied and I am prompted to authenticate.
