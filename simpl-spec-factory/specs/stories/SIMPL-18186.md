# SIMPL-18186: Automatic publication of vocabularies UI
- Status: Ready for DG Acceptance

- Created: 2025-09-10, updated: 2026-09-25

## Description and acceptance criteria

**As a** Governance Authority user,\
**I want** to have a new vocabulary or vocabulary version automatically become available after successful validation\
**So that** I do not require a manual publication action

### *Acceptance Criteria*

*AC1 - Automatic publication after successful validation*\
**Given** a newly submitted vocabulary or vocabulary version has passed all required validations\
**When** the vocabulary is stored in the system\
**Then** the system automatically sets its status to ACTIVE
