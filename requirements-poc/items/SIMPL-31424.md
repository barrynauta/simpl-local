# SIMPL-31424: Governance Authority maintains a contact email for each participant

**L1 requirement (high-level requirement)** · Status **Internal QA check**

## What this item is

This L1 requirement makes the Governance Authority keep a contact email for every participant, visible to the other participants, and lets participants change it after onboarding.

## Requirement

> Simpl shall allow the Governance Authority to maintain a registry of the contact emails for all the participants, making these emails available to other onboarded participants in the system. Simpl shall allow all participants to update their contact email address after onboarding is completed.
