## What this item is

This is the Jira item for business process [BP02A Configure ID/Trust security solution](https://code.europa.eu/simpl/simpl-open/foundations/business-processes/-/blob/main/bp02-configuration-governance-authority/bp02a-configure-idtrust-security-solution.md). In BP02A the Governance Authority sets up identification and trust for its data space: identity attributes, onboarding procedure templates and security settings. Requirements for BP02A hang under this item.
